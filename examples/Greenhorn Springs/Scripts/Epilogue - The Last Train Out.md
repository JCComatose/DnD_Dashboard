# Epilogue: The Last Train Out
%% Short closing scene after the head wendigo falls. About 20 minutes. %%

## Back in town
> [!read-aloud] Dawn over Greenhorn Springs
> [[Frontier town loop.wav|Town loop: wind, hammering, a dog barking]] By the time you ride back in, the sun is up and the whole town is out on the street. Nobody cheers. They just watch you, the way people watch a storm that went around them.

[[Mabry Calloway]] meets the party at the edge of town with coffee and no questions. [[Eli Rourke|Eli]] and [[Ruth]] sit on the boardwalk outside [[Greenhorn Springs|the saloon]], wrapped in one blanket.

> [!secret] What Mabry knows
> Mabry saw a deer-skulled shape on the ridge three winters ago and told no one. If the party presses, a DC 13 Insight check catches the guilt.

## The reward
[[Saloon loop.wav|Saloon loop: piano, low chatter]] [[Miss Odell]] lays the town's thanks on the bar: 3d10 × 10 dollars in credit, loaded straight onto each [[Credit bracer|bracer]].

If anyone asks what happened at [[Greenhorn Springs (one-shot)#The Den & The Reversion|the den]], roll on the table to see what the town has already heard.

| d6 | Rumor going around town |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | It was a bear. A big one. |
| 2 | [[Eli Rourke\|Eli]] was in on it from the start. |
| 3 | The newcomers are bounty hunters from back east. |
| 4 | The waterfall ran red for an hour. |
| 5 | There are more of those things up north. |
| 6 | Ruth was never missing at all. |

## Trouble at the platform

> He has a letter for "the head" from someone up north. Hold it for the next adventure.

## The last train out
> [!read-aloud] All aboard
> [[Train brakes and steam.wav|Train brakes and steam]] The train sighs into the station right on time, as if nothing happened here at all. [[Reunion sting.wav|Quiet musical sting]] Ruth waves until the platform is gone.
